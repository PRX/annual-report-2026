  - subhead: "PRX is creating shared, community-driven technology that makes the day-to-day work of local public media stations smoother and more financially viable."
  - text: |
      Leaning into quality and care without turning away from new innovations is at the core of PRX’s commitment to the next iteration of media — whether in content creation, product development, or work to support stations. This year, PRX provided noncommercial, community-focused stations free access to our radio and podcast distribution technology — [Networks](https://help.prx.org/hc/en-us/articles/204078934-PRX-Networks-Basics), the [Exchange](https://help.prx.org/hc/en-us/articles/204422880-PRX-Exchange-Overview), and [Dovetail](https://dovetail.prx.org/). These platforms ensure stations around the country can send their unique reporting to regional partners with ease, bolster their budgets through earned revenue on their podcasts, and get the national programs listeners love on their local air without maneuvering through technical barriers.
  - quote:

  - text: |
      PRX kept many services free for more than 347 stations with financial support through [Public Media Infrastructure](https://publicmedia.tech/). PMI is a nonprofit founded by five leading public media organizations, including PRX, coordinating services across the sector in the wake of federal defunding and the closure of the Corporation for Public Broadcasting. Investment from PMI is supporting our tech team’s work to build a brand-safe, vetted ad marketplace. This allows for a network effect, helping station producers generate additional revenue on their podcast content, while protecting listeners who will never have their private data compromised for ad sales. In January, PRX acquired Soundrise, expanding our sponsorship sales capabilities and bringing additional expertise in connecting mission-driven audio with values-aligned advertisers. Soundrise has since been fully integrated into PRX as PRX Sales, creating a unified sales operation across our podcast, broadcast, and digital portfolio.
  - subhead: "We are innovating to build infrastructure that serves all of public media, not just PRX."
  - text: |
      Growing an audience is just as important as monetizing one. This year, PRX launched [Dovetail Spots](https://medium.com/prxofficial/prx-announces-dovetail-spots-enabling-trades-of-promotional-messages-among-shows-ed43f61502b7), which lets shows trade promotional messages with one another. A locally produced podcast can now reach listeners nationwide by swapping promos with like-minded shows, without a large marketing budget. PRX has the capacity to build this type of technology that stations could not easily develop on their own because of investments from individual donors and foundations alike.

      Part of our team’s job is to support public media’s future capacity. Many smaller stations have never had access to technology like dynamic ad injection — a Dovetail feature that allows stations to leverage new sponsors and fundraising opportunities across their catalogue, instead of uploading baked-in ads that quickly become outdated. Others have questions about how to bundle their digital assets — like newsletters, podcasts, and displays — to make the most impact. Some larger stations have relied on expensive commercial technology that isn't designed with public media in mind. PRX works with each partner on a person-to-person level to the benefit of the station, the system, and the listener tuning in at home.
