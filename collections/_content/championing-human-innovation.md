  - subhead: "Listeners deserve stories made with care. Artists who make them deserve a partner who believes in the power of human creativity."
  - text: |
      PRX puts real people at the center of our work — supporting singular talents and building partnerships that bring new perspectives to audiences. A standout example in the last year has been podcaster and writer Avery Trufelman’s [_Articles of Interest_ from Radiotopia](https://www.radiotopia.fm/podcasts/articles-of-interest). Its latest miniseries, “Gear,” captured the zeitgeist with its focus on the history of outdoor clothing and its connection to the military. It also sparked a buzz of its own, with dozens of features in press ranging from [_Harper's Bazaar_](https://www.harpersbazaar.com/fashion/trends/a42710995/articles-of-interest-fashion-podcast-preppy-style-history/) and [_Vogue_](https://www.vogue.in/content/best-podcasts-weve-found-that-dont-give-podcast-bro-energy) to [_The Observer_](https://observer.co.uk/culture/audio/article/how-mens-fashion-began-on-the-battlefield) and [_The Economist_](https://www.economist.com/culture/2025/12/18/the-best-podcasts-of-2025).
  - quote:
      text: "<p>Avery Trufelman is a singular talent in audio. She is one of the most curious, voracious and thorough reporters I've worked with and she has an unmatched ability to be at the tip of the cultural conversation. ‘Gear,’ just like ‘American Ivy’ before it, changed the way that we see the world via the everyday clothing we wear. As she says, her shows are not just for people into fashion they are for everyone because we are all legally required to clothe ourselves. ‘Gear’ was buzzy and wonderful. We're so proud to support her and her work.</p>"

  - text: |
      _Articles of Interest_ is built on things no algorithm can manufacture: a distinctive voice, a point of view, deep reporting, and passion. Work like that takes time, independence, and a support infrastructure behind the scenes that allows the artist to focus on the story and craft behind the episode.
  - quote:

Only a humane, artist-focused nonprofit could allocate resources to such a laborious, time-intensive project like <i>Articles of Interest</i>. Armed with Radiotopia's steadfast support, along with the intelligence and curiosity of our listeners, I'm emboldened to wander down uncharted alleys and discover riveting, undiscovered stories.</p>"
      citation: "Avery Trufelman, Host of <i>Articles of Interest</i>"
  - subhead: "Content can now be made with a few clicks. Quality demands human expertise."
  - text: |
      PRX carries this sensibility of advancing human-centered media in the public interest across our work, whether it’s with one-person production teams or organizations guiding national discourse. Ahead of America’s 250th anniversary in July, PRX Productions worked with the National Constitution Center to create [_Pursuit: A Founders' Guide to Happiness_](https://constitutioncenter.org/news-debate/podcasts/pursuit-the-founders-guide-to-happiness). The series explored what the founders really meant by "the pursuit of happiness,” and featured iconic documentarian Ken Burns, leading scholars, and talented actors bringing historical figures like George Washington and Phillis Wheatley to life.
  - quote:
      text: "<p>We could have just generated voices, people pretending to be George Washington or Phillis Wheatley, but our sensibility is that that would have been wrong. We would never choose to do that.</p>"
      citation: "Genevieve Sponsler, Sr. Director of PRX Productions"
  - text: |
      Upholding human expertise is central, even as we explore the new frontiers technology is bringing us. This March, we started a journey with Fast Forward — an organization empowering nonprofits building tech and AI for humanity — to produce [_Otherwise_, a new podcast exploring AI that makes the world a little better](https://medium.com/prxofficial/fast-forward-and-prx-announces-otherwise-a-new-podcast-exploring-ai-for-humanity-f15cb2c99746), and the people who built it that way. We worked closely with Kevin Barenblat and Nicole Davenport, leaders at Fast Forward, to make their debut as podcast hosts, booked compelling guests, and ensured the production, press, and video presence were held to our quality standards.
