  - text: |
      As local radio stations adjusted to new financial realities following the historic defunding of public media, PRX kept fees flat and offered bespoke discounts to stations who needed more support to keep shows on air. PRX’s model is a membership model: membership allows PRX to build the support systems and services that benefit stations and listeners, and program fees for individual shows send revenue directly back to the production partner. This year, we also offered nine shows free of charge to all PRX members, giving stations more programming options for their listeners, without an additional financial burden.
  - quote:
      text: "<p>We're giving smaller, more rural stations more choice on the broadcast distribution side, so they are able to find more shows that are available to them, many at no cost.</p>"
      citation: "Chad Lampe, Director of Public Media Partnerships"
  - text: |
      It’s not just stations who benefit from PRX’s infrastructure. Independent producers, like composer Tyler Kline, who hosts and produces [_Living Classical_](https://www.looseleaftransmissions.com/), can regularly hear his show on more than a dozen stations nationwide. Through the PRX Exchange, our original online marketplace for public radio distribution, _Living Classical_ is available to stations in the United States free of charge, so they can diversify their airtime on a weekly, monthly, or periodic basis, and cultivate the next wave of listeners.
  - quote:

  - text: |
      This year, we began distributing three new shows to audiences nationwide: _Click Here_, _Health Wanted_ and _Sports in America_. With breakout growth in carriage of _The Sam Sanders Show_, _No Small Endeavor_, _Embodied_, _Left, Right and Center_, and _Sports in America_ — and steady listenership across our entire portfolio — PRX programming reached more than 5.4 million listeners weekly across 840 stations, reach that goes even farther with repeater signals. When audiences find PRX programming on the radio, they tend to keep listening. Nearly 60% of the audience for programs like _This American Life_, _The Moth_, and _Living On Earth_ stays tuned for the whole broadcast. Our exceptional programming makes for exceptional listener dedication that far outpaces commercial radio, and our shows are some of the fastest growing in public media.
