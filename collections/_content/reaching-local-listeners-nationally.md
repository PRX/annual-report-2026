  - text: |
      As local radio stations adjusted to new financial realities following the historic defunding of public media, PRX kept fees flat and offered bespoke discounts to stations who needed more support to keep shows on air. PRX’s model is a membership model: Membership allows PRX to build the support systems and services that benefit stations and listeners, and program fees for individual shows send revenue directly back to the production partner. This year, we also offered a series of free shows to all PRX members, giving stations more programming options for their listeners, without an additional financial burden.
  - quote:

  - text: |
      It’s not just stations who benefit from PRX’s infrastructure. Independent producers, like Tyler Kline, who hosts and produces [_Living Classical_](#TK), can regularly hear his show on more than a dozen stations nationwide. Through the PRX Exchange, our original online marketplace for public radio distribution, _Living Classical_ is available to stations in the United States free of charge, so they can diversify their airtime on a weekly, monthly, or periodic basis, and cultivate the next wave of listeners.
