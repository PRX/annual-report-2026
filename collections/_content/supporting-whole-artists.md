  - subhead: "PRX curates partners, not shows."
  - text: |
      At every juncture, across every department at PRX, our efforts go beyond serving the needs of the content to supporting the success of the individuals behind the work. We are not just concerned with contracted deliverables to mix, market, distribute, or produce a show. We invest in artists and teams as the people who are the foundation and future of public-interest media.

      Behind every show in PRX’s curated portfolio is work that listeners may never see, but adds value they can certainly hear: time spent developing concepts, coaching hosts, and helping creators clarify the “why” that makes their final product so impactful for their specific audience. That care extends to every team we work with, from first-time hosts to station producers who have been connected to PRX for years.
  - quote:
      text: "<p>Oftentimes, people have creative ideas and don't know how to bring them to life. Maybe it's a multi-platform franchise, maybe it's a brand extension. So, for me, the diamonds look like more offerings to stations and the public writ large that make an impactful difference in the lives of people. That diamond is content that you can't find anywhere else.</p>"

  - subhead: "We invest in creators’ success — with us and beyond our scope."
  - text: |
      PRX partnerships are long-term. Creators often return for second and third projects because they value our staff’s expertise and commitment to quality. Defector Media’s Alex Sujong Laughlin worked closely with the Radiotopia team this year to figure out her next career move after handing off [_Normal Gossip_](#TK), a proud member of Radiotopia, to new host Rachelle Hampton and producer Se'era Spragley Ricks. Alex and Defector launched two new shows with PRX since then: [_Try Hard_](#TK) and [_Only if You Get Caught_](#TK), hosted by Patrick Redford.

      Other relationships have spanned more than a decade, like Radiotopia’s work with Hrishikesh Hirway, and [_Ear Hustle_](#TK)’s Earlonne Woods and Nigel Poor. Hrishikesh’s _Song Exploder_ hit its [milestone 300th episode this year, featuring Lady Gaga](#TK). Radiotopia has also been home to his podcasts _Home Cooking_ and _The West Wing Weekly._ We care about amplifying creators like Hrishikesh, Earlonne, and Nigel in their careers beyond podcasts, too. This year, PRX supported Hrishikesh’s debut at Tribeca Festival, where he was the first ever Official Selection for music. Beyond the 17 seasons and 88 million downloads, Nigel and Earlonne have written a book, launched a new media center inside California Institution for Women, and this year visited 19 cities across the US with Ear Hustle Live! We recognize the creative lives of the artists we work with are bigger than any one project, and celebrate their success whether that work is with PRX directly or beyond our scope.
