2026 at PRX

Public-interest media is entering a new era. At PRX, we spent the last year growing our capacity to respond to new challenges, new needs, and new opportunities — taking care to center the people at the core of this work. We deepened partnerships with creators, put free tools into the hands of local stations, and strengthened our commitment to human-led storytelling for listeners everywhere. PRX is building the next generation of media in the public interest. We have done it before, when we were at the vanguard of digital audio more than two decades ago. We continue to lead today because of and for you — our listeners, supporters, and partners. Thank you for helping us build the future of public media.
